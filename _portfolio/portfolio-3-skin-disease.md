Led development of a skin disease detector app during a 24-hour Google DSC hackathon, using TensorFlow Lite for real-time classification. Preprocessed and augmented the Harvard skin disease image set with OpenCV and tuned the model to 82% accuracy. The Android app classifies diseases through the smartphone camera and locates the nearest dermatologist. [Source on GitHub](https://github.com/Divy2000/Skin-Diseases-Detection-Hackbash)
